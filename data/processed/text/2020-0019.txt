THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2020-0019, In the Matter of Charles Porrier and
BingBing Li, the court on April 1, 2021, issued the following
order:
The motion filed by the respondent, BingBing Li, “to transfer the trial court
recording of telephonic hearing on 03/12/21,” is hereby denied. Having
considered the briefs and record submitted on appeal, the court concludes that
oral argument is unnecessary in this case, see Sup. Ct. R. 18(1), and that the
appealing party, the respondent, has not established reversible error, see Sup.
Ct. R. 25(8); see also Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740 (2014).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Hicks, Bassett, Hantz Marconi, and Donovan, JJ.,
concurred.
Timothy A. Gudas,
Clerk
