BASSETT, J. Father appeals an order of the Circuit Court (Mace, J.)
terminating his parental rights over his minor child, R.H., on the ground that
he failed to correct, within 12 months, the conditions that led to the circuit
court’s finding under RSA chapter 169-C that R.H. was neglected by R.H.’s

III (2014). This appeal presents a narrow question: when does the 12-month
