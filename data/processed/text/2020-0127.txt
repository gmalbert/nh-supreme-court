THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2020-0127, State of New Hampshire v. Loretta
A. Obi, the court on March 18, 2021, issued the following order:
Having considered the briefs and record submitted on appeal, the court
concludes that oral argument is unnecessary in this case, see Sup. Ct. R.
18(1), and that the appealing party, Loretta A. Obi, has not established
reversible error, see Sup. Ct. R. 25(8); see also Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737,
740 (2014).
Affirmed.
Hicks, Bassett, Hantz Marconi, and Donovan, JJ., concurred.
Timothy A. Gudas,
