THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2025-0523, In the Matter of Jennifer Sabin and
Carl Cicchetto, the court on September 29, 2026, issued the
following order:
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted on
appeal, and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See Sup. Ct.
R. 20(3). The respondent, Carl Cicchetto, appeals a final order of the Circuit
Court (Cross, J.), issued following a hearing, declining, among other things, to
order the petitioner, Jennifer Sabin, to pay child support relative to their children
in common. See RSA chapter 458-C. Having considered the respondent’s briefs,
the memorandum of law filed by the petitioner, and the record on appeal, we
affirm the trial court’s order. See Sup. Ct. R. 25(8).
Affirmed.
Donovan, Countway, Gould, and Will, JJ., concurred.
