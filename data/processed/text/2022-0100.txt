THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2022-0100, Donato J. Dandreo, III & a. v. Town
of Salem & a., the court on October 18, 2022, issued the
following order:
Having considered the briefs and record submitted on appeal, the court
concludes that oral argument is unnecessary in this case, see Sup. Ct. R.
18(1), and that the plaintiffs, as the appealing parties, have not established
reversible error, see Sup. Ct. R. 25(8); Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740
(2014).
Affirmed.
Hicks, Hantz Marconi, and Donovan, JJ., concurred.
