THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2023-0354, Leonard John LaPadula, III v. Paula
Degen & a., the court on February 14, 2025, issued the following
order:
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted on
appeal, and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See Sup. Ct.
R. 20(3). The plaintiff, Leonard John LaPadula, III, appeals an order of the
Superior Court (Kissinger, J.) granting the motion of the defendants, Paula
Degen, Paulette Yeaton, Walter Maroney, Elizabeth Vanlandingham, George N.
Copadis, New Hampshire Employment Security, and Karen Levchuk, to dismiss
the plaintiff’s civil complaints against them relative to their involvement in
processing the plaintiff’s application for certain federal pandemic unemployment
assistance benefits. The plaintiff argues that the trial court erred by dismissing
his claims on the grounds that they are barred by sovereign immunity, see RSA
541-B:1, II-a(a) (2021), and that RSA chapter 282-A provides the exclusive
remedy, see RSA 282-A:68 (2023). As the appealing party, the plaintiff has the
burden of demonstrating reversible error. Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740
(2014). Based upon our review of the trial court’s decision, the plaintiff’s
challenges to it, the relevant law, and the record submitted on appeal, we
conclude that the plaintiff has not demonstrated reversible error and affirm the
court’s decision. See id.; Sup. Ct. R. 25(8).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Bassett, Donovan, and Countway, JJ., concurred.
