THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2021-0233, In the Matter of Ashleigh Lombardo
and Gregory Lombardo, the court on October 28, 2022, issued
the following order:
Having considered the briefs and record submitted on appeal, the court
concludes that oral argument is unnecessary in this case, see Sup. Ct. R. 18(1),
and that the respondent, Gregory Lombardo, as the appealing party, has not
established reversible error, see Sup. Ct. R. 25(8); Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737,
740 (2014).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Hicks, Bassett, Hantz Marconi, and Donovan, JJ.,
concurred.
Timothy A. Gudas,
