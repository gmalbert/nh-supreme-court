THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2021-0490, State of New Hampshire v. Allen E.
Martin, the court on October 5, 2022, issued the following order:
Having considered the brief filed by the defendant, Allen E. Martin, the
memorandum of law filed by the State, and the record submitted on appeal, the
court concludes that oral argument is unnecessary in this case, see Sup. Ct. R.
18(1), and that the defendant, as the appealing party, has not established
reversible error, see Sup. Ct. R. 25(8); Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740
(2014).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Hicks, Bassett, Hantz Marconi, and Donovan, JJ.,
concurred.
