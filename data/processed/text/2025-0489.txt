THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2025-0489, Heidi Gaudreau & a. v. Jay Davey,
the court on July 23, 2026, issued the following order:
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted
on appeal, and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See
Sup. Ct. R. 20(3). The defendant, Jay Davey, appeals an order of the Superior
Court (St. Hilaire, J.), issued following a hearing, finding him in contempt of
the court’s prior order relative to the partition of certain real property, and
ordering him to vacate same. Having considered the briefs and the record on
appeal, we affirm the trial court’s order. See Sup. Ct. R. 25(8). In light of our
decision, the plaintiff’s motion to expedite this appeal is moot.
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Donovan, Countway, Gould, and Will, JJ.,
concurred.
