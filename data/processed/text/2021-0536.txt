THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2021-0536, Paul Ranberg v. HFloesser Realty
NH, LLC, the court on September 16, 2022, issued the following
order:
Having considered the opening and reply briefs filed by the plaintiff, Paul
Ranberg, the memorandum of law filed by the defendant, HFloesser Realty NH,
LLC, and the record submitted on appeal, the court concludes that oral
argument is unnecessary in this case, see Sup. Ct. R. 18(1), and that the
plaintiff, as the appealing party, has not established reversible error, see Sup. Ct.
R. 25(8); Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740 (2014).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Hicks, Bassett, Hantz Marconi, and Donovan, JJ.,
concurred.
Timothy A. Gudas,
Clerk
