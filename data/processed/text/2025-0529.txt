THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2025-0529, William Michaud & a. v. Nathan
Forsaith & a., the court on February 27, 2026, issued the
following order:
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted on
appeal, and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See Sup. Ct.
R. 20(3).1 Defendant Aimee Forsaith appeals an order of the Circuit Court (Kane,
R., approved by Gorman, J.) denying her motion for late entry of counterclaims in
a small claim matter upon which judgment had previously been issued and
reconsideration denied. Because the defendant did not timely appeal the trial
court’s decision on the merits, nor its order on reconsideration, we issued
procedural orders in this case on September 29, 2025, and October 17, 2025,
limiting the defendant’s appeal to only those issues derived from the trial court’s
decision denying her motion for late entry. On appeal, however, the defendant
challenges only the trial court’s decision on the merits. Accordingly, because
such issues are not properly before us, and because the defendant has not
developed any legal argument relative to the trial court’s decision denying her
motion for late entry, we will not disturb the trial court’s order. See State v.
Blackmer, 149 N.H. 47, 49 (2003) (confining our review to only those issues that
have been fully briefed); In the Matter of Birmingham & Birmingham, 154 N.H.
51, 56 (2006) (observing that self-represented litigants are bound by the same
procedural rules that govern parties represented by counsel).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Donovan, Countway, and Gould, JJ., concurred.
Timothy A. Gudas,
Clerk
1 In reaching our decision, this court has not considered the appendix filed by the plaintiff on
