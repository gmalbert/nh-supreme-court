THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2025-0299, Laurie Ortolano v. City of Nashua &
a., the court on June 17, 2026, issued the following order:
The plaintiff’s motion for leave to file a corrected citation and argument is
granted. The court deems paragraph VI.A. on page 51 of her brief to have been
replaced with the language set forth in paragraph 4 of her motion.
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted on
appeal, and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See Sup. Ct.
R. 20(3). The plaintiff, Laurie Ortolano, appeals orders of the Superior Court
(Smith, J.): (1) dismissing her lawsuit pursuant to RSA chapter 91-A against the
defendants, the City of Nashua, its mayor, and several of its attorneys and
officials; (2) granting the defendants’ requests for sanctions; (3) awarding the
defendants $3,998 in attorney’s fees; and (4) denying a post-appeal motion to
disqualify one of the city’s attorneys. Having reviewed the plaintiff’s brief, the
defendants’ memorandum of law, and the record on appeal, we affirm the trial
court’s orders. See Sup. Ct. R. 25(8).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Donovan, Countway, Gould, and Will, JJ.,
concurred.
