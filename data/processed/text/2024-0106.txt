THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2024-0106, State of New Hampshire v. Glenn
Caron, the court on December 19, 2024, issued the following
order:
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted
on appeal and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See
Sup. Ct. R. 20(2). The defendant, Glenn Caron, appeals a finding of guilty,
following a bench trial in the Circuit Court (Alfano, J.), on a violation-level
charge of driving an unregistered vehicle. See RSA 261:40 (2024). On appeal,
the defendant raises numerous challenges to the finding, including arguments
that the trial court lacked jurisdiction, that the registration requirement of RSA
261:40 did not apply, and that RSA 261:40 violates the Federal Constitution.
As the appealing party, the defendant has the burden of demonstrating that
the trial court erred. Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740 (2014). Based upon
our review of the record, including the appendices and the transcript of trial,
the defendant’s arguments, and the relevant law, we are unpersuaded by the
defendant’s arguments and conclude that the defendant has not demonstrated
that the trial court erred. See id.
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Bassett, Donovan, and Countway, JJ., concurred.
