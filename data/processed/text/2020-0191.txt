THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2020-0191, In the Matter of Paul Hunt and
Rachael Hunt, the court on February 25, 2021, issued the
following order:
Having considered the briefs and record submitted on appeal, the court
concludes that oral argument is unnecessary in this case, see Sup. Ct. R. 18(1),
and that the appealing party, Paul Hunt, has not established reversible error, see
Sup. Ct. R. 25(8); see also Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740 (2014).
Affirmed.
Hicks, Bassett, Hantz Marconi, and Donovan, JJ., concurred.
