THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2025-0595, State of New Hampshire v. Shawn
Kinney, the court on March 24, 2026, issued the following order:
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted on
appeal, and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See Sup. Ct.
R. 20(3). The defendant, Shawn M. Kinney, appeals his convictions, following a
bench trial in Circuit Court (Cross, J.), on one misdemeanor count of driving a
vehicle with a suspended registration, see RSA 261:178 (2024), and one violation
count of driving after revocation or suspension, see RSA 263:64 (Supp. 2024).
On appeal, the defendant argues that the trial court erred by “proceeding [with
the case against him] without first establishing jurisdiction after jurisdiction was
timely challenged.” We affirm.
Here, the trial court did not proceed with the case before first resolving the
defendant’s challenge to jurisdiction. Rather, the record demonstrates that the
trial court heard the defendant’s arguments on the issue, as well as the State’s
response, and then expressly found that it had jurisdiction before proceeding
with the case. Accordingly, we discern no error. Further, to the extent that the
defendant’s brief can be construed to advance the argument — although
inadequately developed, see State v. Blackmer, 149 N.H. 47, 49 (2003) — that the
trial court erred by finding that it had jurisdiction in this case, we disagree. See
RSA 625:4, :7 (2016); RSA 502-A:1, VIII, :11 (Supp. 2025); RSA 490-F:3 (Supp.
2025); RSA 594:14 (Supp. 2025).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Donovan, Countway, Gould, and Will, JJ.,
concurred.
