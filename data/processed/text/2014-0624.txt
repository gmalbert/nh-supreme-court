THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2014-0624, State of New Hampshire v. Alain
Lanciaux, the court on September 10, 2015, issued the following
order:
Supreme Court Rule 22(2) provides that a party filing a motion for
rehearing or reconsideration shall state with particularity the points of law or fact
that he claims the court has overlooked or misapprehended.
We have reviewed the claims made in the defendant’s motion for
reconsideration. It appears from the record that the defendant preserved his
challenge to the sufficiency of the evidence when he filed a motion to set aside the
verdict. Accordingly, we modify our August 10, 2015 order by: (1) deleting the
first two full paragraphs on page 2 and moving the following sentence to begin
the next paragraph: “On appeal, the defendant first argues that the evidence does
not support his conviction.”; and (2) deleting the words “plain error” from the
ninth line of page 3 of the same order.
In his motion for reconsideration, the defendant addresses: (1) the
sufficiency of the evidence; (2) the jury instructions; (3) alleged prosecutorial
misconduct due to the prosecutor’s decision not to call a witness listed on
witness disclosure list, but see Super. Ct. Crim. R. 62, III, 13 (requiring
disclosure of names of witnesses who may be called) (emphasis added); (4) the
length of jury deliberations; and (5) the defendant’s failure to provide this court
with a complete record, see Sup. Ct. R. 13 (2).
We have reviewed each of these issues and, upon reconsideration, we
affirm our August 10, 2015 order and deny the relief requested in the defendant’s
motion.
Order modified;
reconsideration denied.
Dalianis, C.J., and Hicks, Conboy, Lynn, and Bassett, JJ., concurred.
