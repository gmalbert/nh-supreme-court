THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2026-0096, Eastman Community Association v.
Noah Gabriel, the court on May 14, 2026, issued the following
order:
The defendant’s request that this court order the transfer of the trial court
record in this case is denied for failure to show cause why providing a copy in an
appendix would be impracticable or inadequate for appellate review. See Sup. Ct.
R. 13(4); In the Matter of Birmingham & Birmingham, 154 N.H. 51, 56-57 (2006)
(observing that procedural rules are not relaxed for self-represented parties).
The court has reviewed the written arguments and the record submitted on
appeal, and has determined to resolve the case by way of this order. See Sup. Ct.
R. 20(3). The defendant, Noah Gabriel, appeals an order of the Circuit Court
(Christo, J.) entering default judgment in favor of the plaintiff, Eastman
Community Association, in a small claims action. The defendant was defaulted
for his failure to answer the claim. On appeal, the defendant advances several
arguments challenging the trial court’s decision, primarily arguing that he did
not receive constitutionally adequate notice of the action.
As the appealing party, the defendant has the burden of demonstrating
reversible error. Gallo v. Traina, 166 N.H. 737, 740 (2014). The defendant also
has the burden to provide this court with a record sufficient to decide the issues
raised on appeal, as well as to demonstrate that he preserved those issues for
appellate review by first raising them before the trial court. Bean v. Red Oak
Prop. Mgmt., 151 N.H. 248, 250 (2004) (holding that, without such records, we
must assume that the evidence was sufficient to support the result reached by
the trial court); see also Birmingham, 154 N.H. at 56-57. Based upon our review
of the trial court’s order, the defendant’s challenges to it, the relevant law, and
the limited record submitted on appeal, we conclude that the defendant has
demonstrated neither reversible error nor that he preserved his arguments for
review, and, accordingly, we affirm the court’s decision. See Gallo, 166 N.H. at
740; Bean, 151 N.H. at 250; Sup. Ct. R. 25(8).
Affirmed.
MacDonald, C.J., and Donovan, Countway, Gould, and Will, JJ.,
