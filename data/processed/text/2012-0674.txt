THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2012-0674, State of New Hampshire v. Robert
Letoile, Jr., the court on August 12, 2014, issued the following
order:
Construing defense counsel’s July 7, 2014 letter to Supreme Court Clerk
Eileen Fox as a motion for clarification, the court waives the requirements of
Rule 22 and grants the relief requested. The second sentence of the paragraph
beginning at the bottom of page 8 of the court’s May 16, 2014 opinion shall be
deleted and replaced with the following language:
He also contends that the trial court erred in not
suppressing the search warrant because Conway’s affidavit
contained irrelevant information regarding the defendant’s alleged
prior assault of his ex-wife and alleged prior sexual assaults of his
ex-wife’s two daughters.
Request for clarification
granted; opinion modified.
Dalianis, C.J., and Hicks, Conboy, Lynn, and Bassett, JJ., concurred.
Eileen Fox,
Clerk
