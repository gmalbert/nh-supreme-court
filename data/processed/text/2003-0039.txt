THE STATE OF NEW HAMPSHIRE
SUPREME COURT
In Case No. 2003-0039, State of New Hampshire v. Daniel Barnes, the court on June 10, 2004,
issued the following order:
The defendant’s motion to reconsider scope of remand is granted. The slip opinion dated April 16, 2004 is
modified as follows: The second to last paragraph of the slip opinion is deleted and replaced by the following
paragraph.
On remand, the trial court should order a new trial if the records exist, unless the State proves that the
defendant’s inability to use the records in the first trial was harmless beyond a reasonable doubt. See Amirault,
149 N.H. at 545. We cannot presently determine whether the trial court’s error was harmless beyond a
reasonable doubt because the existence and content of the records are unknown.
Broderick, C.J., and Nadeau, Dalianis, Duggan and Galway, JJ., concurred.
